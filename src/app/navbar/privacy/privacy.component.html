This notice explains what Anglers Secret stores about you and what you can do about it. It is written to meet our obligations under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who is responsible

Anglers Secret is run by an individual as a hobby project. The data controller can be contacted at [ADD YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

What we store

  • Your username and email address, which you provide at signup.
  • Your password, stored only as a bcrypt hash. We cannot read it or recover it.
  • The dates your account was created, last updated, and last signed in.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers, and we do not build a profile of you.

Why we store it, and our lawful basis

We hold this data to provide you with an account, which is our performance of a contract basis under Article 6(1)(b). The email address specifically lets us identify your account and contact you about it, including resetting your password or telling you about a security incident. We will not send you marketing.

Weather searches

When you search for fishing conditions, the location you enter is sent to WeatherAPI to retrieve the forecast. Those searches are not linked to your account or stored by us. Please avoid entering a precise home address.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Accounts that have not been signed in to for {{ dormantAccountMonths }} months are treated as dormant and deleted. If you delete your account yourself, it is removed immediately and permanently.

How it is protected

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored or logged in readable form.
  • Sign-in attempts are rate limited to frustrate brute-force guessing.
  • Secrets are held in server environment configuration, never in the source code.
  • Error logs record messages only, so your details do not end up in them.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and portability — download everything we hold from your account page.
  • Erasure — delete your account and its data from that same page.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct anything inaccurate, using the contact address above.
  • Objection and restriction — ask us to stop or limit how we use your data.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Browser storage

When you sign in we store a single sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for, so it does not require a cookie consent banner under PECR. Signing out removes it.
